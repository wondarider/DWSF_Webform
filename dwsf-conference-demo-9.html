About DWSF

The Digital Wellbeing Security Framework (DWSF) is a practical maturity assessment designed to help organisations understand and strengthen digital wellbeing. Inspired by proven cybersecurity approaches, DWSF provides a structured way to identify strengths, uncover gaps, and build more resilient digital environments.


This demonstration showcases a simplified version of the framework only covers Governance and Education and provides a snapshot of your current digital wellbeing maturity.


The full framework goes further, covering students, educators, and parents in depth and produces a benchmark score, a actionable uplift project roadmap and an Executive report.
